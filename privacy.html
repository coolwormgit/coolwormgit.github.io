Privacy Policy

OTT-X — IPTV player for LG webOS · Last updated: 14 September 2026

In one paragraph: OTT-X has no user accounts and no servers of ours. We do not collect, see, sell or share your data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. Everything you configure lives on your own device. The only optional transfer of your data off the device is the Google Drive backup, which you switch on yourself, which goes to your own Google Drive, and which is encrypted on the device with a PIN before it is uploaded — we can never read it.

1. Who we are

OTT-X is an independently developed IPTV/OTT player application for LG webOS televisions (and a companion mobile app sharing the same core). In this policy, "the app" means OTT-X, and "we" means its developer. The app plays streams that you supply through a provider or playlist you already have; we provide no channels, content or playlists.

2. Data we collect

None. We do not operate any backend service for the app, and the app sends us nothing. There is no OTT-X account, no sign-up, no telemetry, no crash reporting to us, no usage statistics and no advertising identifier.

3. What the app stores, and where

All of the following is stored locally on your device and never leaves it unless you enable the backup described in section 5:

  • the IPTV provider details you enter (server address, username, password) or your playlist URL;
  • your channel order, category order, favourites, hidden channels and channel logo overrides;
  • your EPG sources and the channel-to-guide mappings you make;
  • app preferences such as language, font size and sorting;
  • if you enable the Drive backup, a cryptographically derived verifier that lets the app recognise the correct PIN. This is not the PIN and not a decryption key, and it cannot be used to decrypt anything.

Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

4. Network connections the app makes

OTT-X talks only to endpoints that are necessary to do what you asked, and only to destinations you control or that are inherent to the feature you enabled:

Video streams are played directly between your device and your provider. We are never in the middle and cannot see what you watch.

5. Google Drive backup (optional, off by default)

If you choose Back up now and sign in with Google, the app uploads a single file containing a dump of the app settings listed in section 3.

What we ask Google for

The app requests exactly one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This grants access to only the files and folders the app itself creates. The app cannot see, read, modify or delete any other file in your Drive, and it cannot see your email contacts or anything else about your Google account.

Where it goes

Into a folder named OTT Play Backups in your own Google Drive. It is not sent to us and there is no copy on any server of ours.

How it is protected

  • The backup is encrypted on your device, before upload, using AES-GCM with a 256-bit key derived from a PIN you enter, by PBKDF2-SHA256 with 120,000 iterations.
  • Neither Google nor we ever see your PIN or the contents of the backup. Without the PIN the file cannot be opened by anyone, including us.
  • The sign-in uses Google's OAuth device flow. Your Google password is typed on Google's own page, never in the app, and the app never receives it.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps the 10 most recent backups in that folder and deletes older ones it created. You can delete the folder or any file at any time in Google Drive yourself, and the app's Disconnect option stops using the connection and forgets the stored token. To revoke the app's access entirely, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove OTT-X.

6. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone — we do not have it.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs or device fingerprinting.
  • We do not build profiles of what you watch.
  • We do not require an account, an email address or any personal detail to use the app.

7. Security

Backups are end-to-end encrypted with your PIN as described above, and the app stores only a PIN verifier rather than the PIN or any key. Communication with Google and with your provider uses HTTPS. As with any application, the security of your provider credentials also depends on the physical security of your device: the settings are stored locally, so treat the TV itself as you would any device holding a password.

8. Children

OTT-X is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

9. Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability over personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or erase. The data on your device is under your direct control: you can change it in the settings, and remove it entirely by uninstalling the app or by deleting the backup folder from your Google Drive.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it, and the current version always lives at this address. Material changes affecting the backup feature will also be described in the app's release notes.
