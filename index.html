OTT-X

A fast, remote-first IPTV player for LG webOS. Built to be driven entirely from the remote you already have — no shortcuts to memorise.

Privacy policy Google Drive backup

What it is

Everything from the remote

Arrow keys, OK, Back, the coloured buttons and 0–9. Categories on the left, channels on the right, with a sliding menu and no hidden key combos.

Xtream Codes and M3U

Sign in with a provider, or load a playlist. Large lists are ordered and preloaded so the list stays responsive while you scroll.

EPG that is actually right

XMLTV guide data, mapped per channel or per category, with program times taken from your device clock so nothing drifts.

Channel logos

Picons are matched automatically, with a per-channel override when the automatic match picks the wrong one.

Live TV, and VOD

The live player watches for drift and rebuffering and quietly resets itself, and the media library browses your provider's movies and series.

Your layout, remembered

Channel order, favourites, categories, EPG mappings and logos are yours — reorder them once and they survive restarts and reinstalls.

Encrypted backup to your own Google Drive

If you turn on Google Drive backup, OTT-X uploads one file — a dump of your app settings — into a folder in your own Google Drive.

  • It is encrypted on the TV, before it is uploaded, with a PIN you choose (AES-GCM 256, key derived with PBKDF2-SHA256 at 120,000 iterations).
  • Only the app's own folder is ever touched. The requested Google scope is drive.file, which grants access to nothing else in your Drive.
  • We operate no servers. There is no OTT-X account, and no analytics of any kind.

Installing

OTT-X is distributed as a webOS package (.ipk) and installed on the TV with the webOS CLI over Developer Mode. A companion mobile app talks to the same core.

ares-install -d tv OTT-X_2.0.0_webOS.ipk
ares-launch  -d tv com.example.ottplay
